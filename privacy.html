Threadbare Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026
Published at: https://mustafayevt.github.io/threadbare-site/privacy.html

Threadbare: Doll Survivors is a game for iPhone, iPad and Android made by Tural Mustafayev ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is involved when you play and what choices you have.

1. The short version

  • No account is needed. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We run no server. Your progress is saved on your own device.
  • The game shows optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob. When ads are on, Google's ad software collects some device and usage information to show and measure ads. This is the only third-party data collection in the game.
  • If you buy Remove ads, the ad software is not started at all.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google Play, and leaderboards by Apple Game Center or Google Play Games. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your information, and the game does not ask iOS for permission to track you.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves unlocked dolls and maps, upgrades, best times, settings and a run in progress on your device, and remembers whether you own Remove ads. We do not receive this information. It is deleted when you delete the app. Your device's own backup (such as iCloud or Google backup), if you use one, may include it under Apple's or Google's terms.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

Threadbare shows rewarded ads only: short ads you choose to watch for a reward. Ads never play in the middle of a fight. The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK, and when it runs Google may collect, under its own disclosures:

  • an approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • device and advertising identifiers (on Android the Advertising ID; on iOS an app or vendor identifier);
  • information about your interactions with ads and the app, such as ad views and taps;
  • diagnostic information such as crash logs and performance data.

Google uses this to deliver and measure ads, limit repeats and prevent fraud, under Google's privacy policy (see also how Google uses information from apps). We ask Google to show only ads suitable for a general audience (rated PG or lower).

iPhone and iPad: the game never shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency request, so iOS gives the ad software no advertising identifier (IDFA), and we do not track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device's settings.

Remove ads: if you own it, the ad software is not started and none of the above takes place.

4. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before any ad is requested. The form appears on the Home screen, never during a run. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy button in the settings on the Home screen. Your choice is stored on your device by Google's consent software.

5. Purchases

There is one optional purchase, Remove ads, processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your name, payment card or billing address. The store tells the game only whether you own the purchase, and the game remembers that on your device. Restore it on a new device with the Restore button in the settings. See Apple's privacy policy and Google's.

6. Leaderboards

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center (iPhone, iPad) or Google Play Games (Android), the game sends your best survival time on each map to that service's leaderboards. Your score is shown with your Game Center or Play Games player name to other players under that service's settings. We do not receive your account details. Leaderboards are optional: if you are not signed in, nothing is sent.

7. What we do not do

  • We use no analytics or crash-reporting services of our own.
  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or camera.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information.
  • We run no server that stores information about you.

8. Children

Threadbare is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, limited to content suitable for a general audience. If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, contact us through the link below.

9. How long information is kept

On your device until you delete the game or its data. By Google, Apple and Google Play according to their own policies. By us: we hold no information about you; messages you post as issues are public on GitHub and are governed by GitHub's terms.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no personal information about you, most requests are best made to Google, Apple or Google Play through their privacy tools. You can always change your ad consent in the game, buy Remove ads, reset your device's advertising identifier or limit ad personalisation in device settings, or delete the game to delete everything it saved. For any question or request, contact us as below.

11. Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling information differently, we will update this policy and its effective date.

12. Contact

Tural Mustafayev. Open an issue at https://github.com/mustafayevt/threadbare-site/issues; replies are posted there. Please do not include personal information, as issues are public. See also the support page.
