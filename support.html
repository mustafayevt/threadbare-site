Threadbare support

Threadbare: Doll Survivors is made by Tural Mustafayev.

Contact

To report a problem, ask a question or send feedback, open an issue at https://github.com/mustafayevt/threadbare-site/issues. Replies are posted there. Please do not put personal information in an issue: it is public.

Helpful to include: your device and OS version, the game version (shown in the game's settings) and what happened.

Common questions

  • Restore Remove ads on a new device: use the Restore button in the game's settings, signed in to the same Apple or Google account.
  • Ads: ads are optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob. Remove ads turns them off.
  • Progress: saves are kept on your device only. Deleting the game deletes them.
